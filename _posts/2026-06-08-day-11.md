what_i_learned: |
  Today our research team made a significant pivot in the direction of our project. We discussed the need to align our work with the requirements of a specific research conference, which prompted us to reconsider our original approach and think about the project from a new perspective. This shift encouraged us to reevaluate our goals and adjust our methodology to better fit the expectations of a formal research submission.

  As part of this pivot, we spent time brainstorming new ideas and exploring different ways to approach the problem. This process helped promote more autonomy within the group, as each team member was able to think independently while still contributing to a shared direction. I learned how important flexibility is in research, especially when adapting to new requirements or constraints.

  We also began exploring new non-communicable diseases (NCDs) to identify additional insights and potential research opportunities. This exploration is helping us broaden the scope of our understanding and may lead to new findings that can strengthen our project.

  In addition, we started planning a completely new design document that reflects our updated direction. This step is helping us restructure our project from the ground up to ensure it aligns with our new research goals and conference expectations.

blockers: |
  One challenge today was adjusting to the pivot in our research direction. Shifting away from our previous plan required us to rethink much of what we had already developed. It also took time to understand how to reshape our project so that it aligns with conference expectations while still building on our previous work.

reflection: |
  Today was an important reminder that research is an iterative and flexible process. Although pivoting our direction required us to rethink our previous progress, it also opened up new opportunities for exploration and learning.

  I appreciated the collaborative brainstorming session because it allowed everyone to contribute ideas and take ownership of the new direction. Working more independently while still being part of a team helped me better understand how research environments balance autonomy and collaboration.

  Overall, today helped me become more comfortable with change in a research setting and more confident in adapting to new goals. I am looking forward to developing our new design document and exploring the new direction of our project further.
