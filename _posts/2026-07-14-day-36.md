what_i_learned: |
  Today I took the lead on creating our project demonstration video. I was responsible for filming, directing, and editing the video to showcase our research and explain our project in a clear and engaging way. It was exciting to step into a creative role while still contributing to our research.

  As we recorded the video, I learned that presenting technical information for a general audience is different from writing a research paper. We had to think carefully about how to explain our project in simple terms while still highlighting the key ideas and the work our team has accomplished.

  After editing the video, I uploaded the final version so it would be ready to share. Seeing the finished product come together after filming, editing, and reviewing it was rewarding and gave me a sense of accomplishment.

  Overall, today's experience helped me develop skills beyond programming and research. It reinforced that effective communication and presentation are important parts of sharing scientific work with others.

blockers: |
  One challenge today was coordinating the filming process and making sure everyone was comfortable with their speaking parts. Editing the video also required careful attention to detail to ensure the final version was polished before uploading.

reflection: |
  Today was one of my favorite days because I had the opportunity to be creative while supporting our project. I enjoyed directing, editing, and publishing the demo video and seeing everyone's contributions come together into one final product.

  The experience reminded me that research is about more than collecting data and building models. Being able to communicate our work effectively through different formats is just as important, especially when presenting to audiences who may not have a technical background.

  Overall, today helped me strengthen my communication, leadership, and creative skills. I am proud of the final video and excited for others to see the work our team has accomplished.
