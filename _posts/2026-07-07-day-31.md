what_i_learned: |
  Today our team spent time reviewing additional research papers related to our project. Reading more of the literature helped us better understand how other researchers have approached similar problems and what methods have been successful in previous studies.

  As we discussed each paper, I learned how important it is to compare different approaches before deciding on a methodology. Each paper offered a unique perspective, and together they helped us gain a broader understanding of the field and how our project fits into the existing research.

  We also talked about how the findings from these papers could strengthen our own research paper. Looking at how other researchers organized their work, explained their methods, and presented their results gave us ideas for improving our own writing.

  Overall, today's literature review helped reinforce the importance of building research on a strong foundation of existing knowledge and evidence.

blockers: |
  One challenge today was reading through several papers and identifying the most relevant information for our project. Each paper contained different methods and findings, so it took time to determine which ideas would be the most valuable to incorporate into our work.

reflection: |
  Today reminded me that research is a continuous learning process. Every paper we read provides new ideas, different perspectives, and a better understanding of the work that has already been done in the field.

  I enjoyed discussing the papers with my teammates because everyone noticed different details and contributed valuable insights. Working together made it easier to understand the material and think critically about how it applies to our project.

  Overall, today strengthened my understanding of our research area and gave me more confidence as we continue developing our project and completing our research paper.
