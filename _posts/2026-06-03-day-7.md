what_i_learned: |
  Today began with a deeper discussion about the T1GRANDA dataset and its role in our research project. We explored why this dataset was selected and how its contents align with the goals of our study. Learning about the reasoning behind the dataset selection helped me better understand the foundation of our research and how important high-quality data is when conducting meaningful analysis.

  A significant portion of the day was dedicated to collaborating with other undergraduate researchers in my group. Together, we brainstormed and developed approximately sixteen potential research questions related to our project. This process required us to think critically about what questions would be both meaningful and feasible to investigate. We evaluated different ideas and considered how each question could contribute to the overall objectives of our research.

  After generating our list of questions, we met with our graduate mentor to discuss several of our strongest candidates. During this discussion, we explained why we believed each question would be effective for our research paper and received feedback on their potential impact and scope. These conversations helped me better understand how researchers refine ideas and narrow broad interests into focused research questions.

  Later in the day, I had the opportunity to view several Figma designs created by other students in the program. It was interesting to see the creativity and design approaches used by my peers. I also submitted my own Figma project, which was a recreation of the EA Games website. Completing this project allowed me to practice web design principles and improve my skills in creating professional-looking user interfaces.

  To conclude the day, I focused on finishing my Gradio projects. Completing these applications gave me additional experience working with interactive AI interfaces and strengthened my understanding of how front-end components connect with Python functionality. Finishing the games also gave me a sense of accomplishment because I was able to see my projects working from start to finish.

blockers: |
  One challenge today was narrowing down potential research questions. While generating ideas was relatively straightforward, determining which questions would be the most impactful and practical for our research required careful consideration and discussion. However, receiving feedback from our graduate mentor helped provide direction and made the decision-making process easier.

reflection: |
  Today highlighted the importance of both collaboration and planning in the research process. Working with my peers to develop potential research questions showed me how valuable it is to hear different perspectives and ideas. The discussions with our graduate mentor helped me understand that strong research begins with asking thoughtful and focused questions.

  I also enjoyed seeing the Figma projects created by other students because it exposed me to different design styles and creative approaches. Submitting my EA Games website replica was rewarding because it reflected the effort I put into learning design tools and recreating a professional website layout.

  Completing my Gradio games was another major accomplishment. It felt rewarding to finalize projects that combined programming, problem-solving, and user interaction. Overall, today strengthened my understanding of research design, collaboration, and software development, while also giving me confidence in the progress I have made throughout the program.
