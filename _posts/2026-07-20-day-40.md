  Today our team focused on finalizing the figures we created in Google Colab for our research paper.   Working on the figures helped me realize that data visualization is an important part of communicating research. A well-designed figure allows readers to quickly understand the results without having to read through large amounts of text. Small improvements in presentation can make a big difference in how effectively research is shared.

  By the end of the day, we had polished our figures and felt confident that they clearly supported the story we wanted to tell through our research.
