what_i_learned: |
  Today our team reflected on the progress we have made since pivoting our project. After spending time reviewing research papers, cleaning data, and exploring datasets, we officially confirmed our new research direction.

  One of the key things I learned was how important dataset selection is to a research project. We discovered that the GEO HumanMDD dataset did not contain dysthymia data, which was necessary for our original goal of comparing disorders and identifying common biological patterns. This finding helped us understand why a pivot was needed and reinforced the importance of carefully evaluating data sources.

  We also continued discussing how our new direction aligns with the research we have reviewed through literature studies. These discussions helped connect our findings from the papers to the goals of our updated project and design document.

  As a team, we reviewed our progress and identified next steps for moving forward. It was encouraging to see how much clearer our project has become compared to when we first began exploring the pivot.

blockers: |
  One challenge was adjusting our project after realizing that our original dataset did not contain all of the information we needed. This required additional time for literature reviews, dataset exploration, and discussions to ensure that our new direction would better support our research goals.

reflection: |
  Today helped me appreciate how research often requires flexibility and adaptation. While changing directions was initially challenging, it ultimately helped our team develop a stronger and more realistic project.

  Looking back at the work we have completed, including literature reviews, data cleaning, and design document development, I can see how each step contributed to helping us make informed decisions about our project.

  Overall, I feel more confident about our new direction and excited to continue building on the foundation we have established. The experience has taught me that setbacks can often lead to better opportunities for learning and growth.
