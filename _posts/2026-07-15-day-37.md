what_i_learned: |
  Today our team focused on drafting the methodology and results sections of our research paper. We worked together to clearly explain the methods we used throughout the project, including our data preparation process, model development, and evaluation approach. Writing these sections helped me realize how important it is to accurately document every step of the research process.

  We also spent time organizing and describing our model results. Rather than simply presenting numbers, we discussed how to explain what the results mean and how they relate to our research question. This helped me better understand the importance of interpreting results so that readers can understand the significance of our findings.

  Throughout the meeting, we collaborated in Overleaf to refine our writing and ensure that each section flowed logically. It was rewarding to see our paper continue coming together after weeks of conducting experiments, reviewing literature, and analyzing data.

blockers: |
  One challenge today was deciding how to present our methodology and results in a way that was both detailed and easy to follow. We wanted to include enough information for readers to understand our work without making the sections overly complicated.

reflection: |
  Today showed me that writing a research paper is just as important as conducting the research itself. Clearly explaining our methodology and interpreting our results helps others understand our work and makes our findings more meaningful.

  I also enjoyed collaborating with my teammates as we worked through different sections of the paper. Everyone contributed ideas that helped improve the overall quality of our writing.

  Overall, today was another productive step toward completing our research paper. It feels rewarding to see everything we've worked on throughout the summer come together into a polished final document.
