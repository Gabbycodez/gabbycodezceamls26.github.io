what_i_learned: |
  Today our team completed a full run-through of our research presentation. Practicing together gave us the opportunity to see how the presentation flowed from beginning to end and identify areas where we could improve our delivery. We also made our final decisions on slide content, speaking order, and the key points each team member would cover.

  Going through the presentation helped me realize how important preparation is before presenting research. Practicing allowed us to refine our explanations, improve transitions between speakers, and make sure our main findings were communicated clearly.

  By the end of the day, we felt more confident in our presentation because everyone understood their role and the story we wanted to tell through our research.

blockers: |
  One challenge today was deciding what information to keep and what to leave out. We wanted to include enough detail to explain our work while keeping the presentation within the time limit and easy for the audience to follow.

reflection: |
  Today's practice showed me that a successful presentation takes teamwork and preparation. Running through the slides together helped us catch small improvements that we might have missed if we had only reviewed them individually.

  It was rewarding to see how all of our work over the summer—from exploring datasets and building models to writing the paper and creating figures—came together into one final presentation. I left today's meeting feeling more prepared and excited to present our research.

  Overall, today was a great milestone as we finalized our decisions and prepared for the final presentation with confidence.
