what_i_learned: |
  Today I spent time learning about Signal-Gated Omic Fusion (SGOF), the machine learning approach that our project is evaluating. We walked through how the model combines information from multiple types of biological data while reducing the influence of data sources that do not contribute meaningful information.

  One of the most interesting concepts I learned was how the model evaluates each type of biological data separately before combining them. Instead of treating every dataset equally, the model assigns more weight to the data that performs well on validation data and less weight to data that does not provide useful predictive information. This approach helps improve the reliability of the final predictions.

  I also learned more about graph convolutional networks and how they represent relationships between patients by creating patient similarity graphs. Understanding how these graphs are built and how they are used to generate embeddings helped me better understand the overall architecture of our project.

  Overall, today's discussions helped me connect many of the concepts we have been learning throughout the program. I now have a better understanding of how our proposed model differs from more traditional machine learning approaches and why it may produce stronger results.
