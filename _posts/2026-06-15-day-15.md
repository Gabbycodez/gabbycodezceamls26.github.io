what_i_learned: |
  Today began with a discussion led by Dr. Riggs about AI bias and its impact on technology and society. We explored how bias can enter AI systems through the data used to train models, the assumptions made during development, and the ways technology is applied in the real world. This conversation helped me better understand the importance of creating AI systems that are fair, responsible, and inclusive.

  The discussion encouraged me to think more critically about the role developers and researchers play in reducing bias and ensuring that technology serves diverse groups of people. It also reinforced the idea that building effective AI systems involves more than technical skills; it also requires ethical awareness and thoughtful decision-making.

  Later in the day, we participated in an interactive teamwork workshop. Our challenge was to build the tallest possible tower using only marshmallows and noodles. Although the materials were simple, the activity required creativity, planning, communication, and collaboration.

  Through the workshop, I learned the importance of listening to teammates, testing ideas, and adapting when things do not go as planned. The challenge demonstrated how teamwork and communication can help a group solve problems more effectively and achieve a common goal.

blockers: |
  One challenge during the workshop was keeping the tower stable as it became taller. Our group had to rethink parts of our design and make adjustments throughout the activity. While some of the concepts discussed during the AI bias session were complex, the conversation helped me gain a better understanding of the topic.

reflection: |
  Today was both engaging and informative because it combined discussions about responsible technology with hands-on teamwork. The conversation with Dr. Riggs expanded my understanding of AI bias and reminded me that technology can have real impacts on people and communities.

  I also enjoyed the tower-building challenge because it provided an opportunity to work closely with others and practice collaboration in a fun environment. Watching different teams develop unique solutions showed that there are often many ways to approach the same problem.

  Overall, today helped me strengthen both my understanding of ethical issues in AI and my ability to work effectively as part of a team. These are skills that will be valuable throughout my research experience and future career.
