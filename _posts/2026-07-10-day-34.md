what_i_learned: |
  Today I spent time rerunning several of our machine learning models using different parameters to test whether our hypothesis about the data was correct. Adjusting the model settings allowed us to compare the results and determine whether the changes improved performance or supported what we expected to see. This process helped me better understand that developing machine learning models often involves experimentation and repeated testing rather than finding the right answer on the first attempt.

  As we reviewed the new results, I learned how small changes in model parameters can affect performance. Comparing the outcomes helped us determine whether our theory was supported by the data and gave us additional insight into how our models behave.

  Later in the day, we attended our weekly research presentations where each group shared updates on their projects. It was interesting to see how other teams have progressed over the past week and to learn about the different challenges and accomplishments they experienced. Watching their presentations also gave me ideas for how we can continue improving our own project and presentation skills.

blockers: |
  One challenge today was determining whether the changes in model parameters truly improved performance or if the differences were simply due to normal variation. Interpreting the results required careful analysis and discussion before drawing conclusions.

reflection: |
  Today reinforced that research is an iterative process. Rather than expecting every experiment to produce the desired outcome, I learned that testing different approaches is an important part of finding the best solution. Each experiment, whether successful or not, helps us better understand our data and models.

  I also enjoyed attending the weekly presentations because they reminded me that every research team is making progress in different ways. Seeing everyone's work motivated me to continue refining our project and learning from both our successes and our challenges.

  Overall, today strengthened both my understanding of machine learning experimentation and my appreciation for the collaborative research environment within the program.
